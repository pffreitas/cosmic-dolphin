Graham’s recipe for great work: choose a field you are deeply curious about, learn enough to reach its frontier, notice the gaps, and explore the promising ones.

  • Curiosity first. Let real interest choose the field. It misleads you less than fashion or money.
  • Own projects. Keep an ambitious project of your own going, however small it starts.
  • Consistency. A little done every day compounds. Great work is rarely one burst.
